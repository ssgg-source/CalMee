# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in CalMee a harassment-free experience for everyone,
regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, constructive, and specific.
- Assume good intent while addressing disagreements directly.
- Respect privacy. Do not share recordings, transcripts, credentials, or personal data.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and help repair their impact.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, deliberate intimidation, publishing
private information, and other conduct inappropriate in a professional community are
not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit, remove, or reject contributions and participation that
violate this policy. A private enforcement contact will be published before the first
public release.

This policy is adapted from Contributor Covenant 2.1:
https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/
